**HAI2LAB** (Human-AI Interaction & Intelligence Lab) is led by **Jiwoong Heo**, Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Statistics at Chosun University (since September 2026).

Our research lies at the intersection of **mobile sensing**, **human–computer / human–vehicle interaction**, and **applied large language and vision-language models**. Current directions include:

- **Transportation Mode Detection (TMD)** from smartphone and GPS sensing, and its application to mobility and quality-of-life analysis
- **Human–Vehicle Interaction**, including take-over requests (TOR) in automated driving
- **LLM/VLM applications** such as traffic accident fault-ratio prediction and video content classification

Before joining Chosun University, Jiwoong was a postdoctoral researcher at GIST (Department of AI Convergence) and received his Ph.D. in Computer Software from Hanyang University.

**We are recruiting** motivated undergraduate and graduate students. If you are interested in our research, please email with your CV and a brief statement of interest.
