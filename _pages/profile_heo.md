**Jiwoong Heo** is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Computer Statistics at Chosun University and the director of HAI2LAB. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Software from Hanyang University (2023) and was a postdoctoral researcher at KIST and GIST before joining Chosun University in September 2026.

His research focuses on **transportation mode detection** from mobile sensing, **human–vehicle interaction** in automated driving, and **applied LLMs/VLMs** for traffic accident analysis and content classification.
