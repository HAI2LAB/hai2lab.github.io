As of September 1, 2026, HAI2LAB (Human-AI Interaction & Intelligence Lab) has officially started at the Department of Computer Statistics, Chosun University.

Our research focuses on transportation mode detection from mobile sensing, human–vehicle interaction, and applied large language and vision-language models.

We are recruiting motivated undergraduate and graduate students. If you are interested, please reach out by email with your CV and a brief statement of interest.
